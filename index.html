Additional Tips for Dining Area

    Recommendations

  • Immediately clean up spills to prevent stains.
  • Use microfiber cloths for streak-free wiping.
  • Check table corners for leftover items.
  • Rotate decorative items for thorough dusting.
  • Disinfect high-touch areas (door handles, etc.) regularly.

    Things to Avoid

  • Using too much water on wooden surfaces.
  • Skipping daily trash removal.
  • Ignoring seat cushions or booth crevices.
  • Using harsh chemicals that might damage finishes.
  • Leaving floors wet without signage.

Additional Tips for Kitchen

    Recommendations

  • Label and date all food containers.
  • Regularly check fridge temperatures.
  • Clean under large appliances monthly.
  • Sterilize cutting boards frequently.
  • Use color-coded cloths for different tasks.

    Things to Avoid

  • Using harsh chemicals near open food.
  • Neglecting hard-to-reach corners.
  • Overlooking filters in extraction fans.
  • Allowing grease to build up on surfaces.
  • Storing cleaning agents near food supplies.

Top Recommendation

Regularly disinfect high-touch surfaces.

Thing to Avoid

Using the same cloth for multiple surfaces.

Additional Tips for Toilets

    Recommendations

  • Keep a separate mop for restroom floors.
  • Use disinfectants that target common restroom bacteria.
  • Check for leaks in plumbing regularly.
  • Always stock air fresheners if possible.
  • Wipe door handles and locks after each cleaning session.

    Things to Avoid

  • Mixing cleaning chemicals (dangerous fumes).
  • Overlooking ventilation fans.
  • Ignoring the underside of toilet seats.
  • Using abrasive pads that scratch surfaces.
  • Leaving a wet floor without a caution sign.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

1. What happens after I submit the checklist?

After you fill in and submit the checklist, a copy of the completed report is sent to the designated email address you provided. You’ll see all your selected tasks marked as done (or pending) along with any comments you added. This helps you maintain a clear record of your cleaning activities for reference or auditing.

2. How do I reset the checklist?

At the bottom of the checklist page, there is a Reset Checklist button (marked with an “X” symbol). Clicking this button clears all marked tasks, comments, and any uploaded photos. It instantly brings the checklist back to its original blank state, so you can start fresh for your next cleaning session.

3. How can I switch between Standard and Deep Cleaning tasks?

Each area (e.g., “Kitchen,” “Offices,” “Dining Area,” etc.) has a toggle button at the top. When you click it, you’ll switch from Standard cleaning tasks to Deep cleaning tasks (and vice versa). The button text updates accordingly—so if you’re viewing Standard tasks, it’ll say “Switch to Deep Cleaning,” and if you’re viewing Deep tasks, it’ll say “Switch to Standard Cleaning.”

4. Can I upload photos, and how does that work?

Yes. Each area has a large, pulsing Upload Photo button that is the full width of the form to draw your attention. When clicked:

  • On a phone, it typically opens your camera by default (though this depends on your device settings).
  • On a desktop, it opens a file selection dialog.
If you successfully select or snap a photo, the button text changes to say “Photo Ready for Analysis,” and you’ll see the file name appear below. If you decide you no longer want to send the photo, there’s a Remove Photo button which clears it.

Note: If you’re using the free tier, uploading photos is not allowed. Any uploaded photo will cause the form to redirect you to the subscription page instead of submitting.

5. What if I have tasks that are different or need to be removed for a while?

Our system’s “smart list” approach is designed to adapt based on your previous submissions:

  • Standard tasks that are marked as done might be hidden for 24 hours (in more advanced setups).
  • Deep tasks might not show again for 14 days if marked done previously.
This means that tasks you’ve recently completed may not reappear until enough time has passed. If you have a unique scenario or need a custom task interval, please contact us to discuss your subscription options.

6. I’m on the free tier. How many submissions can I make?

Under the free tier, you’re allowed 50 submissions per month. If you exceed that limit, the form automatically redirects you to our subscription page. Also, photo uploads are disabled under the free tier.

7. What if I exceed my free tier limit or need photo analysis?

Once you reach 50 submissions in a month—or if you try to upload photos while on the free plan—you’ll be redirected to our subscription page, where you can upgrade and enjoy additional features, including photo-based AI suggestions.

8. How do I add comments or notes for each task?

Below each task’s “Mark Done” button, there’s a Remarks box (textarea). You can type in any observations, additional cleaning steps, or special notes. These comments appear in the final report email and help keep track of important details.

9. Where do I find the progress bar, and how does it work?

You’ll see a progress bar fixed at the top of your screen. As you mark tasks “Done,” it automatically updates, showing how much of your checklist is completed. This visual feedback helps motivate your team to finish all tasks.
